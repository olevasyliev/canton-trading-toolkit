Pricing both venues…

Cantex and Tradecraft, quoted at four trade sizes every five minutes. Each figure is what you would actually receive, after every fee the venue publishes.

The paper router

Every five minutes it places a $1,000 CC/USDCx order, alternating buy and sell, and fills it wherever it returns more. It runs on live quotes and executes nothing.

Cross-venue spread scan

Buy a token on one venue and sell it on the other at every size and pair. A route is listed only when it comes back at least $0.50 ahead after fees.

Same tokens, matched on the ledger. Pairs are paired by Canton instrument (issuer party and id), not by ticker. Only pairs that trade on both venues appear.

Tradecraft is priced from its public pool reserves with the venue's own formula, which reproduces its quote API to the digit. It publishes no network fee, so none is taken off.

Cantex is priced by its own quote API. Its flat network fee (about 1.24 CC a swap) is taken off the output, which is why the smallest trades lose a little there.

Sizes in dollars are converted at the Tradecraft CC/USDCx mid. The cost on the chart is measured against the average of the two venues' mid prices.
