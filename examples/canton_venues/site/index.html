Every token on Canton's decentralised exchanges, priced live from the pools.

Heatmap

Tile size is liquidity or volume, colour is the price change. Click a tile to compare venues for it.

Canton premium

The same asset priced on Canton's DEXes against its outside market. Positive means Canton pays more. Dots show each venue on its own.

Alerts

Fired when a condition starts: a stablecoin more than 0.5% off $1, an asset more than 1% from its outside price, a token moving more than 5% in an hour, or a cross-venue route clearing its costs. Pools under $20K are ignored.

Best execution

What each venue returns at four trade sizes, pool fees and price impact included, network fees excluded on both sides.

Paper desk

Two strategies run every five minutes on live prices. Nothing is executed, and the whole record is public, losses included.

Liquidity pools

Fee income to liquidity providers, annualised from the last 24 hours of volume. Rewards and price moves are not included.

Open API

Every number on this page is a JSON file anyone can read. No key, no sign-up, cross-origin enabled, refreshed every five minutes.


  


  

    
Canton Venues
Built on canton_toolkit, open source. Read-only: nothing here is signed or executed.

    
Method
Tokens are matched by Canton instrument (issuer party and id). Cantex and Tradecraft are priced from live reserves with each venue's own formula.

    
Data
Cantex and Tradecraft public APIs, DefiLlama for ecosystem volume and TVL, CoinGecko for outside prices and logos. Temple and Rocky need a key and are not priced yet.
