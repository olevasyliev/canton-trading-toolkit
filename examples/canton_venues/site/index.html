Canton's prices against the rest of the market

The same asset, priced on Canton's DEXes and on its outside market. A positive number means Canton pays more. Dots show each venue on its own, so a gap between them is a spread you can see.

CantexTradecraft

Where a trade fills better

Both venues priced from their live reserves at four sizes, with every pool fee taken off. Network fees are left out on both sides.

A paper desk, trading the numbers above

Two strategies run every five minutes on live prices, with nothing executed. Their whole record is public, losses included.

Router

A $1,000 CC/USDCx order every tick, alternating buy and sell, sent to whichever venue returns more.

Spread desk

Buys a token on one venue and sells it on the other at the size that pays most, but only after deducting 3 CC for network costs and clearing $0.50.

What liquidity providers earn

Fee income to LPs, annualised from the last 24 hours of volume. Rewards and price moves are not included.

All of it is open data

Every number on this page is a JSON file anyone can read. No key, no sign-up, cross-origin enabled, refreshed every five minutes.
