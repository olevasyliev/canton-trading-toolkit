Canton premium

The same asset priced on Canton's DEXes against its outside market. Positive means Canton pays more. Dots show each venue on its own.

Alerts

Fired when a condition starts: a stablecoin more than 0.5% off $1, an asset more than 1% from its outside price, a token moving more than 5% in an hour, or a cross-venue route clearing its costs. Pools under $20K are ignored.

Best execution

What each venue returns at four trade sizes. Tokens that also trade on an order book get a second view in dollars, with every venue on one chart.

Perpetuals

Every perp market on Rocky and Ekiden. Basis is the perp price over the outside spot price (CoinGecko; CC against its Canton price). Ekiden basis uses its mark price.

Paper desk

Two strategies run every five minutes on live prices. Nothing is executed, and the whole record is public, losses included.

Liquidity pools

Fee income to liquidity providers, annualised from the last 24 hours of volume. Rewards and price moves are not included.

Open API

Canton Venues
Built on canton_toolkit, open source. Read-only: nothing here is signed or executed.

    
Method
Tokens are matched by Canton instrument (issuer party and id). Cantex and Tradecraft are priced from live reserves with each venue's own formula, Rocky from its order books. A token's Canton price weighs each venue by the dollars it can trade within 1% of its own mid. For an order book, liquidity is the dollars resting within 1% of mid.

    
Data
Cantex, Tradecraft, Rocky and Ekiden public APIs, DefiLlama for ecosystem volume and TVL, CoinGecko for outside prices and logos. Temple, the largest venue, needs a key and is shown by volume only.
